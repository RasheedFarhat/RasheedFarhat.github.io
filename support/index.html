Ticket path

From report to resolution.

  1. 01

    Clarify the impact

    Capture what changed, who is affected, and what the user was trying to do when the issue appeared.

  2. 02

    Isolate the cause

    Reproduce the problem where possible, check the likely hardware, software, configuration, and connectivity layers, and record the result.

  3. 03

    Resolve or route

    Apply the fix and guide the user through it, or send the right specialist the symptoms, tests, results, and reproduction steps.

  4. 04

    Verify and document

    Confirm service is restored, record the remedial action, and update shared references when the issue is likely to recur.
